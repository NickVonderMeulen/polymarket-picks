Market makers (two-sided books, very high volume, near-certain bets) are dropped, and the rest are ranked by settled-bet edge, not raw profit. Score = trader quality (15) + wallet agreement (20, wallets that trade alike count once) + entry price (15) + recent buying (15) + conviction (10, bet size vs the wallet's own median bet) + low opposition (10) + execution (15). These weights are starting guesses, not proven settings. Prices are resting limit buys (no taker fee), never more than 3c above their entry; if the market has moved past that, the pick is below the line. Not financial advice.
