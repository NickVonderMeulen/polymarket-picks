Market makers (two-sided books, very high volume, near-certain bets) are dropped, and the rest are ranked by settled-bet edge, not raw profit. Score = trader quality (15) + wallet agreement (20, wallets that trade alike count once) + entry price (15) + recent buying (15) + conviction (10, bet size vs the wallet's own median bet) + low opposition (10) + execution (15). These weights are starting guesses, not proven settings. Prices are resting limit buys (no taker fee), capped a few cents above their entry; if the market has moved past that, the pick is below the line. NFL and NBA picks are also checked against sportsbook odds (vig removed) and only count as bets when the sportsbooks agree the price is low; other markets have no outside check and stay below the line.
